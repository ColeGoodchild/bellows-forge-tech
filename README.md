# Growth Forge

Build a modern, premium website for a company called GrowthBellows.

Brand positioning:

GrowthBellows is an AI-powered growth systems company that helps small and medium-sized businesses grow through websites, SEO, automation, and AI-powered business tools.

Core tagline:

"Forging Growth Through Technology"

Brand story:

A blacksmith does not create strength from nothing — they use heat, tools, and precision to transform raw material into something valuable. GrowthBellows brings that same philosophy to businesses by providing the technology, systems, and automation needed to transform their online presence and generate sustainable growth.

The brand should feel:

- Premium

- Modern

- Technical

- Trustworthy

- Craftsmanship-oriented

- Like a high-end engineering company, not a typical marketing agency

Avoid:

- Generic marketing agency aesthetics

- Loud startup gradients

- Cartoon AI imagery

- Cheap Fiverr-style design

- Overly corporate consulting feel

Visual identity:

Use a refined industrial aesthetic:

- Dark charcoal backgrounds

- Warm bronze/copper accents

- Clean white typography

- Subtle references to metal, forging, heat, craftsmanship, and precision

- Modern SaaS-style layouts

- Minimal animations

Create the following pages and sections:

HOME PAGE

Hero section:

Headline:

"Forging Growth Through Technology"

Subheadline:

"We build AI-powered growth systems for small businesses — combining high-converting websites, SEO, automation, and intelligent tools that help you attract more customers and operate more efficiently."

Primary CTA:

"Start Forging Your Growth"

Secondary CTA:

"See Our Services"

Hero visual:

Create a sophisticated visual metaphor of a modern forge:

- glowing metal

- subtle sparks

- precision engineering

- technology integrated with craftsmanship

Do not make it fantasy or medieval. Make it feel like Apple meets industrial design.
